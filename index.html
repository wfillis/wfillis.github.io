Email Alerts

A private tool for one person. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Sends its owner scheduled email notifications, such as stargazing weather forecasts.
  • Holds email sent to the owner's two personal holding Gmail accounts and delivers it to the owner's main Gmail inbox early on Saturday or Sunday morning.

It runs on the owner's own server and is used only with the owner's own Google accounts.
